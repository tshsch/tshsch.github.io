如何写中文博客

VSCode界面右下角编码方式从GBK改为UTF-8

The default encoding used by Jekyll is UTF-8. Jekyll expects all files to be in this format and may produce errors if they are not.

UTF-8 and GBK Key Differences:

Language Support: UTF-8 covers almost all Unicode characters globally.GBK mainly covers Chinese and ASCII. 

Encoding Structure:
UTF-8: ASCII uses 1 byte; most European/Middle Eastern characters use 2 bytes; Chinese characters typically use 3 bytes.

GBK: ASCII characters use 1 byte; Chinese characters use 2 bytes.
